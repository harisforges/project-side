## Decision

### Kit contents: the fur roller is out
The plan's kit was slicker + steel comb + reusable fur roller. **The roller is removed from the base kit.**

New hero kit: the **"Takusut 3-Step Long-Hair Kit"**:
1. Self-cleaning slicker brush, long-hair spec (from decision 01): **detangle**
2. Two-sided steel comb, wide + fine teeth, about 19 cm: **check for hidden knots**
3. Small fine-tooth face comb, about 10 cm: **face, paws, behind the ears**

The steps are printed on the box and insert card: *1 Brush → 2 Comb → 3 Finish*.

**What this means:**
- **First 120 days:** if the Kickstart waiver applies, we're well above RM10.
- **Without the waiver, at 16.74%:** the kit makes about RM8.48. That is still above the Day-45 gate's RM8 floor, but below the RM10 validation rule.
- **The fix, in order:**
  1. Push landed cost to RM9.50 or less with a sea reorder. Sea freight is about 4–6x cheaper per kg than air.
  2. Hold blended ads at 10% or less.
  3. If both fail by Day 60, raise the list price to RM44.90.
- I'm **not** raising the price before launch. RM39.90 is the gap price from decision 01, and the gates will tell us if it converts.

### Break-even ROAS (ad order, no affiliate, worst case)
Contribution before ads = 39.90 − 11 − 6.68 − 1.51 − 0.54 − 2.12 − 1.20 = **RM16.86**. Break-even ROAS = 39.90 / 16.86 = **2.37**. With 10% Shop Ads affiliate commission: 39.90 / 12.87 = **3.10**.
→ **GMV Max floor raised from 2.4 to 2.6, target stays 3.0.** If the rate turns out to be 11.88%, the floor drops back to 2.4. This lives in `dashboard/config/rules.json`.

## Why (data + reasoning)
- **Roller out:**
  - A full roller weighs about 190 g (decision 01 sources). That breaks the 300 g limit.
  - At about RM21/kg air freight it adds about RM4 freight on top of a ¥8–15 unit cost.
  - It carries ChomChom look-alike risk, and it's for the owner's sofa, not the long-hair matting problem we sell.
  - Comb + face comb completes the groomer routine (brush → comb → finish), weighs about 70 g and costs about ¥3–5.
- **One listing with variations:** reviews, sales count and creator links all build on one product page, and the kit is the default.
- **No fake reference price:** policy and legal risk, and a hard limit in CLAUDE.md.
