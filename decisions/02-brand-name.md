## Why (data + reasoning)

- **The name explains the product to the core buyer in one word.** "Kusut" is the everyday Malay word owners use for matted, tangled fur. Malay-speaking buyers (the majority of TikTok Shop MY) decode "TAKUSUT" instantly. English speakers read it as a distinctive brand. Creators get a built-in hook: *"Bulu kucing kusut? Takusut."*
- **It is a strong mark.** An invented word is far easier to register and defend than descriptive names like "Bulu Lembut" or "Cat Groom Pro". Descriptive marks often get refused at MyIPO, and competitors can copy them.
- **It doesn't resemble existing pet brands.** The searches turned up no pet brand like it. The big names in the space are FURminator, Safari, Catit, Hertzko, Tangle Teezer (Cat Teezer), Me-O and Yasashii, and it is distinct from all of them.
- **Risk I'm managing.** "TAKUSUT" can *sound* Japanese, and Yasashii shows a Japanese feel sells premium. We **must not imply Japanese origin**: no kanji, no Japanese flags, no "Japan quality" copy. That would be a misleading claim (hard limit). The look will be clean Malaysian-modern; item 6 sets it.
- **Separate from EcomForges.** No overlap in name, look or audience.
