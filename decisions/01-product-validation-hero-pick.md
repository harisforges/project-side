# 01 — Product validation & hero pick

Date: 12 Nov 2026 · Status: **PROVISIONAL**. It becomes final after Haris returns the research table (snapshot 1 by 13 Nov, snapshot 2 on 19 Nov).

## Decision

1. **Hero tool: self-cleaning slicker brush, long-hair spec.** It is not a generic one. It must have:
   - pins 15 mm or longer, coated or ball-tipped
   - full pin retraction with a one-push button and no lever
   - a head of about 9–10 cm, sized for cats
   - a weight of 130 g or less
2. **Hero kit stays as the main product**: brush + steel comb + fur roller, at RM39.90. The single brush is a price anchor and entry offer, not the volume driver.
3. **Grooming glove is dropped from the hero shortlist.** It does not detangle long coats or reach mats. It sells at about RM5–10 commodity prices and gives no reason to buy the kit. It can come back later as a low-cost add-on, not a hero.
4. **New backup order:** (1) slicker brush → (2) undercoat dematting/deshedding rake that is *not* shaped like a FURminator → (3) the steel comb sold as a hero ("Persian comb").
5. **Two changes to the validation rules** (the rest of the plan stays as it is):
   - The **price RM19–49** rule is tested on the *kit*, not the single tool. Generic cat brushes on Shopee MY mostly sell at about RM4–18, so a single slicker will rarely clear RM19 on its own.
   - **Packed weight**: the 300 g limit applies to the single SKU. The kit limit is **500 g**, the first weight tier for most MY couriers (an assumption; verified in item 5). A full-size reusable roller weighs about 190 g, so brush + comb + full roller + mailer is about 385 g. That breaks 300 g but fits under 500 g.

## Why (data + reasoning)

- **Demand fit.** Long-haired cats mat. The standard groomer routine is a slicker first, then a comb to check. That routine *is* our kit, so the bundle has a reason to exist beyond "more stuff". A deshedding blade is a short-coat and undercoat tool, and it can cut guard hair on Persians. A glove does not solve matting at all.
- **Market.** Cats are the dominant pet in MY: 77.7% of pet owners own at least one cat (Standard Insights; survey date not stated). That supports volume but does not prove it. Sold counts from Haris's research are the real test.
- **Price reality: this is the biggest risk.** MY roundups and price trackers show Shopee cat brushes at about RM4–18 and a "self-grooming massage brush" up to RM68. Petsupply.my sells Doggyman cat slickers at RM23–32.70. Branded or premium sells at RM23+; generic China slickers sit near RM8–15. So the single at RM19.90–24.90 needs visible spec differences: pin length, coated tips, full retraction, a sturdy build. The kit at RM39.90 is where the margin is.
- **Fixable complaints exist.** Review mining on self-cleaning slickers (Chewy and marketplace reviews of Safari, JW, Petkin, Hertzko-type products) shows repeated complaints:
  - sharp uncoated pins that scratch skin
  - pins that do not fully retract
  - plastic bodies that crack after a drop
  - heads too big for cats
  - two-step button-plus-lever mechanisms

  Every one of these is a supplier spec, so we can fix them at sourcing and say so in the listing. This passes the "1–3 star reviews show fixable complaints" rule, pending MY-specific reviews.
- **Cost.** Indicative China wholesale for self-cleaning pet brushes is about USD 0.8–1.5 per unit (sourcing-agent listings, quantity unclear). That makes the RM10 landed kit cost plausible, but it is **not verified**. Real quotes come in item 4.
- **Look-alike risk.** The FURminator blade shape is heavily copied. We stay away from that silhouette. The fur roller must not copy the ChomChom look (item 4 sets the design rule).

## Confidence

- Category and hero choice (slicker over glove/deshedder for long-haired cats): **medium-high**.
- Demand big enough for 27+ orders/day: **low** until the sold-count snapshots are in.
- Single tool at RM19.90+: **low**. The kit is the bet.

## What would change this decision

| Finding from Haris's table | Action |
|---|---|
| Top 5 slicker listings (Shopee + TikTok combined) have fewer than 2,000 total sold, OR fewer than 2 listings add 50+ sold between snapshot 1 and 2 | Demand too thin → switch hero to dematting rake (#2); re-run the checklist on it |
| No listing with a kit/bundle priced RM30+ has 300+ sold | RM39.90 kit unproven → keep the kit but reprice in item 3 (test RM34.90) |
| Median price of top 10 slickers below RM10 AND top 3 are all overseas (CN-shipped) shops | Pure price war → hero stays, but the single is listed only as a kit add-on, not a standalone SKU |
| 1–3 star complaints are mostly "cat hates it / cat scared" (not fixable) rather than build issues | Weaker differentiation → still go, but the listing leads with a "gentle for sensitive cats" claim only if the pins are coated |
| A dominant local brand holds 3 or more of the top 5 slots with 4.9★ and 5,000+ sold each | Hard to displace → go after the long-hair niche keywords (Persian, Maine Coon, "bulu panjang") rather than the generic term |
