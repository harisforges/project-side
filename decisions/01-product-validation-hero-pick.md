# 01 — Product validation & hero pick

Date: 12 Nov 2026 · Status: **FINAL — GO (13 Nov 2026)**. Snapshot 2 on 19 Nov confirms velocity only. See bottom of file.

## Decision

1. **Hero tool: self-cleaning slicker brush, long-hair spec.** It is not a generic one. It must have:
   - pins 15 mm or longer, coated or ball-tipped
   - full pin retraction with a one-push button and no lever
   - a head of about 9–10 cm, sized for cats
   - a weight of 130 g or less
2. **Hero kit stays as the main product**: brush + steel comb + fur roller, at RM39.90. The single brush is a price anchor and entry offer, not the volume driver.
3. **Grooming glove is dropped from the hero shortlist.** It does not detangle long coats or reach mats. It sells at about RM5–10 commodity prices and gives no reason to buy the kit. It can come back later as a low-cost add-on, not a hero.
4. **New backup order:** (1) slicker brush → (2) undercoat dematting/deshedding rake that is *not* shaped like a FURminator → (3) the steel comb sold as a hero ("Persian comb").
5. **Two changes to the validation rules** (the rest of the plan stays as it is):
   - The **price RM19–49** rule is tested on the *kit*, not the single tool. Generic cat brushes on Shopee MY mostly sell at about RM4–18, so a single slicker will rarely clear RM19 on its own.
   - **Packed weight**: the 300 g limit applies to the single SKU. The kit limit is **500 g**, the first weight tier for most MY couriers (an assumption; verified in item 5). A full-size reusable roller weighs about 190 g, so brush + comb + full roller + mailer is about 385 g. That breaks 300 g but fits under 500 g.

## Why (data + reasoning)

- **Demand fit.** Long-haired cats mat. The standard groomer routine is a slicker first, then a comb to check. That routine *is* our kit, so the bundle has a reason to exist beyond "more stuff". A deshedding blade is a short-coat and undercoat tool, and it can cut guard hair on Persians. A glove does not solve matting at all.
- **Market.** Cats are the dominant pet in MY: 77.7% of pet owners own at least one cat (Standard Insights; survey date not stated). That supports volume but does not prove it. Sold counts from Haris's research are the real test.
- **Price reality: this is the biggest risk.** MY roundups and price trackers show Shopee cat brushes at about RM4–18 and a "self-grooming massage brush" up to RM68. Petsupply.my sells Doggyman cat slickers at RM23–32.70. Branded or premium sells at RM23+; generic China slickers sit near RM8–15. So the single at RM19.90–24.90 needs visible spec differences: pin length, coated tips, full retraction, a sturdy build. The kit at RM39.90 is where the margin is.
- **Fixable complaints exist.** Review mining on self-cleaning slickers (Chewy and marketplace reviews of Safari, JW, Petkin, Hertzko-type products) shows repeated complaints:
  - sharp uncoated pins that scratch skin
  - pins that do not fully retract
  - plastic bodies that crack after a drop
  - heads too big for cats
  - two-step button-plus-lever mechanisms

  Every one of these is a supplier spec, so we can fix them at sourcing and say so in the listing. This passes the "1–3 star reviews show fixable complaints" rule, pending MY-specific reviews.
- **Cost.** Indicative China wholesale for self-cleaning pet brushes is about USD 0.8–1.5 per unit (sourcing-agent listings, quantity unclear). That makes the RM10 landed kit cost plausible, but it is **not verified**. Real quotes come in item 4.
- **Look-alike risk.** The FURminator blade shape is heavily copied. We stay away from that silhouette. The fur roller must not copy the ChomChom look (item 4 sets the design rule).

## Interim verdict: snapshot 1 (13 Nov 2026)

Data: `research/01-product-validation-template.xlsx` and `research/01-tiktok-videos-template.xlsx`, 19 listings and 3 videos uploaded by Haris.

### What the data says
- **Demand exists.** The 19 listings have 22,537 ratings between them. Rating count is used as the proxy here because the sold column was left blank. Assuming 25–40% of buyers leave a rating, that is roughly 55k–90k units over the listings' lifetime. This is an assumption.
- **The price band is far lower than the plan assumed.** Median price is **RM4.60**. Only 3 of 19 listings are RM10 or more, and only **1 of 19 is RM19 or more**: a China-shipped "sikat penyingkiran bulu" at RM26.99 with 573 ratings. Generic search on both platforms is a RM2–10 commodity market.
- **No bundles are proven.** No listing records a bundle price, so the RM39.90 kit thesis has no evidence yet, for or against.
- **The complaints are all fixable, and they are the same ones.** "Poor quality", "broken", "didn't work" and "not as described" appear on 14 of 19 listings. That is the opening for a quality product, but it does not prove that buyers will pay 8x for it.
- **No-face content works.** The top "cat brush" videos are hands and cat only, with 1.3M–15M views. None had a product link and all are 141–292 days old, so there is organic interest but no proven shop video for this product in MY right now.
- **Data quality.** Shopee deshedding #3 and TikTok cat brush #3 are gloves, and TikTok #2 is a wooden comb. Top search results are a mix of product types, which means no dominant slicker brand. That is good for us.

### Decision update
1. **Kill the standalone RM19.90–24.90 single as a Shopee search product.** It would sit at about 4x the median price with no visible reason to pay more. The single exists only as a TikTok kit-page variation (price set in item 3).
2. **Hero is still the slicker, sold as a kit.** We compete on "the set that fixes long-hair matting" and the demo video, not on "a brush". Search buyers expect RM5. Video buyers can be shown why RM39.90 is worth it. This makes TikTok as primary channel even more important.
3. **Kit price floor is RM36.90.** At RM34.90 the kit makes RM9.37/order at RM10 landed cost, which fails the RM10 rule. At RM36.90 it makes RM10.59, and at RM39.90 it makes RM12.43. Below RM36.90 is only viable if real quotes bring landed cost to RM8 or less.
4. **Status: still PROVISIONAL, with confidence in the price/kit thesis lowered to low.** Go/no-go needs the two extra checks below.

### Two gaps to close before 19 Nov
- **Sold counts** (`sold_snapshot1`) for the same 19 listings. Without them there is no velocity reading in snapshot 2.
- **Kit/premium proof.** Five listings priced RM25 or more that are sets or premium brushes. If none show 200+ sold, the RM36.90–39.90 kit is unproven. In that case I will rebuild the offer in item 3 (e.g. RM29.90 kit with cost driven to RM8 or less).

## Update: sold counts added (13 Nov 2026, evening)

- **Demand is much larger than the rating proxy suggested.** 18 listings show **230,593 sold** (TikTok cat brush #2 is blank). That is about 10.9 sold per rating, not the 2.5–4 I assumed. Demand confidence goes to **high**.
- **The highest-priced Shopee slicker is the best seller.** "Self Cleaning Pet Slicker Brush Gentle" (China, RM10.33) has **40,000 sold**, about RM413k lifetime GMV. That is more than double every RM1.69–4.60 listing. Buyers in this category are not just picking the cheapest.
- **Premium proof point.** "Sikat penyingkiran bulu" on TikTok (China, RM26.99) has **3,087 sold**, about RM83k GMV. That is the only evidence above RM19, and it is a single tool, not a kit.
- **Velocity measurement problem.** Shopee rounds big numbers: 5 listings show "10k+", which won't move visibly in 6 days. **Snapshot 2 therefore re-records `rating_count` too.** It is an exact number and grows with sales at about 1 rating per 11 sold.
- **Still missing:** the premium-kit check (5 listings at RM25 or more that are sets or premium brushes, with sold counts). The RM36.90–39.90 kit price is still unproven.

**Decision unchanged:** the slicker is the hero, sold as a kit. Kit price floor stays at RM36.90. Single only as a TikTok variation. Confidence: demand high; price/kit low-medium (up from low because of the RM10.33 and RM26.99 results).
