**Order of work:** TikTok first (primary channel, and review takes longest), then Shopee the same day.

## Why
- **Separation from EcomForges** (CLAUDE.md §2): a different email, phone, bank and brand avoids linking the accounts and keeps the books clean for the profit transfers.
- **Drop-off, 1-day handling:** you pack at home and drop off daily. Pickup windows would tie you to the house.
- **Declared weights:** set them slightly above the real packed weight (rounded up to the next 50 g). Under-declaring triggers weight adjustments and penalties; over-declaring costs shipping.
- **Platform default returns:** extra-generous policies invite abuse on a RM40 item. The defaults already protect buyers properly.

## What would change this decision
- **SSM name ≠ bank account name and the platform rejects it:** open a business account in the SSM name. A Maybank/CIMB SME account online takes 1–3 days. Tell me and I'll push the launch plan.
- **TikTok rejects the category for a new seller:** list under the nearest allowed category and appeal in Seller Center → **Help** → "category application".
- **Tax/legal (I'm flagging, not deciding):** income from a sole-prop SSM is taxed under **your personal income tax** (Form B). Malaysia's e-invoicing rollout has turnover thresholds that likely exempt a business this small. **Ask your EcomForges accountant once (5 min)**: (a) does the e-invoice exemption apply, (b) how to record the profit transfers to EcomForges as a capital contribution or director's loan.

## Haris: exact steps to execute

### A. TikTok business account + TikTok Shop (Sat 14 Nov, about 60 min)
1. **Phone:** TikTok app → Profile → **☰ → Settings and privacy → Switch account → Add account → Sign up**. Use the **takusut Gmail**. Username: `takusut` (fallback `takusut.my`). Name: `Takusut`.
2. In the new account: **☰ → Settings and privacy → Account → Switch to Business Account** → category **Pet** (or "Shopping & Retail").
3. Bio (copy-paste):
   ```
   Long-hair cat grooming. Tak kusut lagi.
   Brush → Comb → Finish 🐾 For Persian, Maine Coon & long-hair cats.
   ```
4. **PC:** open `https://seller-my.tiktok.com` → **Sign up** → use the same takusut Gmail and your phone → verify the codes.
5. Choose **Local seller** → business type **Sole proprietorship / Partnership** (or **Corporation** if the SSM is Sdn Bhd).
6. Upload:
   - SSM certificate (PDF of the registration / e-Info)
   - your IC front and back
   - SSM registration number exactly as on the certificate
7. **Shop name:** `Takusut`. Primary category: **Pet Supplies**.
8. **Warehouse address:** your home address + phone.
9. **Bank account:** Seller Center → **Finance** → **Bank account** → add an account in the SSM name (or yours if sole-prop) → verify.
10. **Link your TikTok account:** Seller Center → **Account** / **LIVE & Video** → **Linked TikTok accounts** → **Link official account** → log in as `@takusut` → approve.
11. **Shipping:** Seller Center → **Shipping** → **Shipping options** → keep platform logistics → handover method **Drop-off** → handling time **1 day**.
12. **Returns:** Seller Center → **Settings** → **Return address** = home address. Leave the policy on default.
13. **Chat:** Seller Center → **Customer Service** → turn on **Auto-reply** (texts in item 9).
14. **Screenshot** the "Under review" status page. Drop it into chat so I know the review clock has started.
15. **Once approved:** screenshot the **commission rate card** (Pet → Dog & Cat Grooming row with headers) and any **New Seller / Kickstart** waiver banner. These confirm item 3.

### B. Shopee shop (Sat 14 Nov, about 40 min)
1. **PC:** `https://seller.shopee.com.my` → **Sign up** with the **takusut Gmail** + phone. Username: `takusut`.
2. **Seller type:** choose **Business/Company** → upload the SSM certificate + IC.
3. **Shop Settings:** **Shop Profile** → Shop name `Takusut` → description (copy-paste):
   ```
   Takusut — long-hair cat grooming tools. 3-step kit: Brush → Comb → Finish. For Persian, Maine Coon and mixed long-hair cats. Ships from KL within 1 working day.
   Takusut — alat dandanan kucing bulu panjang. Set 3 langkah: Sikat → Sisir → Kemas. Dihantar dari KL dalam 1 hari bekerja.
   ```
4. **Shipping:** **Shipping Settings** → enable **SPX Express** → **Drop-off**. Also enable **J&T Express** (drop-off) as backup.
5. **My Addresses:** add the home address → set it as **Default + Pickup + Return** address.
6. **Bank account:** **Finance → Bank Accounts** → add → verify.
7. **Chat:** **Customer Service → Chat Assistant** → turn on **Auto-reply** and **Off-work auto-reply** (texts in item 9).
8. Leave **Vacation mode** OFF. Don't create listings yet. Listings come once stock is in hand (item 6).
