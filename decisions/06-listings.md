## Why
- **Title structure:** brand + product + type + top Malay terms. That covers both English and Malay search (`sikat kucing bulu panjang` and `self cleaning brush cat` were both terms Haris researched in item 1) without keyword stuffing.
- **Kit is the default variation:** the decision 01 data says the single brush can't win on search price.
- **Hands-only visuals:** your no-face constraint. The item-1 research showed no-face cat-grooming videos reaching 1.3M–15M views.
- **No medical/vet claims:** "removes loose undercoat" is factual. "Prevents hairballs" or "cures matting" would be health claims, so avoid them.
