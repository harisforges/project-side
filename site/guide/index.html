TAKUSUT

Your 5-minute long-hair routine

For Persian, Maine Coon and long-haired mixes. Do it 2–3 times a week and knots don't get the chance to form.

The 3 steps

  1. 1

    Brush: detangle

    Use the slicker brush with light strokes in the direction the fur grows. Start at the back and sides, then the chest and tail. Let the pins do the work; don't press down. When the pad is full, press the button and the fur slides off.

  2. 2

    Comb: find hidden knots

    Run the wide side of the steel comb down to the skin. If it catches, stop, go back to the brush for that spot, then try again. Finish with the fine side to smooth the top coat.

  3. 3

    Finish: face, paws, ears

    Use the small comb on the cheeks, under the chin, behind the ears, under the legs and between the toe fur. These are where knots usually start.

How often

CoatRoutine
Persian, Himalayan, very dense coatsEvery day or every other day, 5 minutes
Maine Coon, Ragdoll, long-haired mixes2–3 times a week
Shedding season, or hot humid weeksAdd 1–2 extra sessions

Handling knots safely

  • Hold the fur between the knot and the skin with your fingers so you don't pull the skin.
  • Work the knot from the outside edge in, a little at a time, with the wide comb.
  • Never cut a mat with scissors. Cat skin is thin and easy to cut.

Tight mats close to the skin, mats in the armpits or belly, or any redness or sores: please see a groomer or vet instead of brushing them out.

If your cat isn't a fan (yet)

  • Start with 1–2 minutes, on the back and cheeks only, where most cats enjoy it.
  • Give a treat during and after. Stop before your cat gets annoyed.
  • Same time, same spot each session. Most cats settle into it within a week or two.
  • Watch the tail and ears: a flicking tail or flattened ears means stop for today.

Looking after your tools

  • Press the button to clear fur after every session.
  • Once a week, rinse the pin pad and combs in warm soapy water, then dry fully before storing.
  • If a pin bends, gently straighten it. If the pad is damaged, contact us through the app where you bought it.
