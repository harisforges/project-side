**If stuck**
- **SMS code doesn't arrive** → tap 重新发送 (resend) after 60 s; check the +60 prefix; try on Wi-Fi with mobile data off, then the reverse.
- **Slider/puzzle check (滑块验证)** → drag the slider slowly to the end; on failure, close the app and retry in 10 minutes.

### 2B. Alipay (to pay suppliers)
1. Install **Alipay** (支付宝).
2. Register with **+60** and the business SIM. Complete identity verification with your **passport** if you have one; otherwise follow the IC option if offered.
3. **Me (我的) → Bank Cards (银行卡) → Add card (添加)** → your card from 0.5.

**If stuck (common):** a Malaysian card may get rejected, or verification may need a passport you don't have. **Don't fight it.** Skip Alipay and use the **forwarder's "pay on behalf" service (代付)** in 2D: you pay the forwarder in RM by online banking, and they pay the supplier in ¥ for a fee of about 3–5%. That's the normal way Malaysian small sellers buy on 1688.

### 2C. Find suppliers and send requests (about 75 min)
1. 1688 app → search bar → paste exactly: `长毛猫 自清洁针梳 一键退毛`
2. Tap **筛选 (Filter)** → tick **源头工厂 (source factory)** → Done. Then sort by **销量 (sales)**.
3. Open the top 8 listings one by one. For each, check:
   - shop badge **源头工厂** or **实力商家** ✓
   - shop age **X年** of 3 years or more ✓
   - **回头率 (repeat-buyer rate)** of 20% or more ✓
   - the listing **video** shows a **one push** retracting the pins (no sliding lever) ✓
   - pins look **ball-tipped/coated** in the close-up photos ✓
   - **no brand logo** on the brush ✓
   - the design doesn't copy Hertzko / Safari / FURminator / Pecute ✓

   Keep the **5** that pass the most checks.
4. On each of the 5: tap **客服 / 旺旺 (chat)** → paste this message:
   ```
   您好！我是马来西亚的电商卖家，想采购猫用自清洁针梳（长毛猫用）。
   请确认以下规格：
   1. 针长：15mm或以上（长毛猫用）
   2. 针头：圆珠或涂层，不刮伤皮肤
   3. 一键退毛，按一下针完全缩回（不要拨杆式）
   4. 刷头宽度约9-10cm，整体重量130g以内
   5. 不锈钢针，无品牌logo（白牌）
   采购数量：首单150个，之后每月300-1000个（海运）。
   请报价：150个单价、500个单价、1000个单价，以及发货到广州/义乌仓库的运费和发货时间。
   可以先拍一段按键退毛的视频给我看吗？谢谢！
   ```
5. Search `猫梳 不锈钢 双排梳` → filter 源头工厂 → sort 销量 → pick the **top 3** with ★4.5+ → chat → paste:
   ```
   您好！我想采购两款猫梳：
   A. 不锈钢双排梳（一边宽齿一边密齿），长度约19cm，圆头齿不伤皮肤，无logo
   B. 小号密齿梳（脸部/跳蚤梳），长度约10cm，无logo
   ```

   (Many comb shops sell both A and B. If a shop has only one, also message a second shop for the other.)
6. **6 hours later**, to anyone who hasn't replied:
